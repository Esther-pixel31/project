I’m a full-stack developer who builds innovative solutions for businesses. From house rental management to e-commerce platforms, I design and develop software that makes operations seamless, efficient, and scalable.


Projects I’ve Built

SaaS House Rental System


A property management platform that helps landlords track tenants, payments, and property listings efficiently.

E-commerce Website


Designed and developed a seamless online shopping experience with secure payments and an easy-to-use product catalog.

The Swift Send App, now known as Send App by Flutterwave, is a mobile and web-based platform designed to facilitate fast, secure, and affordable international money transfers.

Monster Collector CLI Game
